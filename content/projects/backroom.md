## Overview

Backroom is the next stage of the product journey that began with [IDLab](/projects/idlab). It explores how the original music-management and DJ workflows can evolve into a more accessible, web-based product.

The project is in development. Its direction builds on what IDLab established while creating room for further work in product design, architecture and functionality.

## The problem

IDLab established useful workflows for downloading, organizing, processing and managing music. Backroom takes the next step: considering how those ideas can be made more accessible through a web-based experience.

## My role

- Evolving the product direction established by IDLab
- Developing the web-based product experience
- Exploring the architecture needed for the next stage of the product
- Refining functionality as the project develops

## Product progression

Backroom evolved from [IDLab](/projects/idlab). They are distinct projects in the same product journey: IDLab is the original foundation, while Backroom develops those underlying ideas in a more ambitious web-based direction.

## What I learned

An existing product can provide a strong foundation without dictating every decision in its successor.

Moving a product into a new environment requires revisiting its experience and architecture, not simply reproducing the original application.
