## Overview

FawarOS is informed by first-hand experience in restaurant operations, and is approached as a product: software meant to be used in a real operating context rather than built as an exercise.

## The problem

Restaurants run on a dense mix of time-sensitive, repetitive work. Small inconsistencies in records, handoffs or routine checks tend to compound over the course of a busy day.

FawarOS addresses that operational layer in general terms: making everyday workflows clearer, more consistent and easier to manage.

## My role

- Defining the product scope from observed operational needs
- Translating business processes into software requirements
- Designing and developing the application
- Testing, refining and iterating on functionality

## What I learned

Requirements are discovered by observing how work actually happens, not assumed in advance. Understanding a workflow has to come before automating it.

In operational software, clarity and reliability matter more than the number of features.

Scoping a product is as much about deciding what not to build as deciding what to build.
